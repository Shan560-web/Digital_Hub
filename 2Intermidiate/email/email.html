E-mail & Online Communication

What is a E-mail

E-mail (Electronic Mail) is a method to send and receive message, files and documents using internet. It’s mostly used in business, education, technical communication and document interactions. In 1971, a test email was sent Ray Tomlinson to himself containing text.

You can send message, assignment or image instantly to another person’s email address like→user123@gmail.com

How to create a email account

To use e-mail, you need a email account with an email service provider like these

  • Gmail → www.gmail.com
  • Outlook → www.outlook.com
  • Yahoo Mail → www.yahoo.com
  • Zoho Mail → www.zoho.com
  • i Cloud Mail → www.icloud.com

Cc (Carbon Copy) & Bcc (Blind Carbon Copy)

1.Cc-Send a copy to another person (everyone can see their address).
2.Bcc-Send a copy secretly (others can’t see who got it).

Sending a Email

To write a email click the compose


  Every email includes:
  • To: Recipient’s address
  • Subject: Short topic (e.g. "assignment")
  • Message Body: The main message
  • Attach: Add files or images (optional)

Writing a Professional Email

  Example

When sending a professional emails:

follow these simple steps

Use a clear subject line

Begin with a greeting (e.g. Dear Sir/Madam)

Write short, clear sentences

Be respectful

End with a closing and your name (e.g. Best regards, "your name")

Safe Email Practices

Do not open unknown attachment→they may contain viruses.

Avoid clicking suspicious links in emails.

Never share passwords or personal details via email.

Use strong passwords for your account.

Log out when using public computers.

Always check the sender’s address before opening messages.


Example

Fake: support@paypa1.com (uses number "1" instead of "l")

Real: support@paypal.com
